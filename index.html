Tap a cell to see its gamma.

Each number is the gamma for one strike (row) and one expiry date (column): roughly how many thousand US dollars of the coin dealers would need to buy or sell if price moved 1%. Blue means they would sell rallies and buy dips, which calms price. Pink means they would do the opposite, which speeds price up. Darker means a bigger number.

Key readings

How to read the heat map

Blue cells: positive gamma

Where the estimate is positive, dealer hedging tends to buy dips and sell rallies. That usually calms price near those strikes.

Pink cells: negative gamma

Where the estimate is negative, hedging tends to follow price in the same direction. That can speed moves up.

How reliable are these numbers?

They rest on an assumption about which side of each trade dealers hold, so treat them as an estimate and not a fact.
